# WordGuess

Three five-letter words, twelve shared guesses. Each guess scores on every board. Casual daily warm-up — no difficulty modes.

Tiles use Operate IQ tokens: `--teal` locked in place, `--amber` in the word but other slot, muted for absent. Keyboard keys show a three-stripe mark, one per board.

On small screens the current guess stays at the top and the keyboard stays at the bottom. The three history boards scroll in the middle. After each guess they re-rank so solved boards move away from the keys and the closest unsolved board snaps down next to the keyboard.

Site nav and heading still collapse behind the play-pull chrome used on sudoku.
