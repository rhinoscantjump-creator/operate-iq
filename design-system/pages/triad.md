# Triad

Three five-letter words, eight shared guesses. Each guess scores on every board.

Tiles use Operate IQ tokens: `--teal` locked in place, `--amber` in the word but other slot, muted for absent. Keyboard keys show a three-stripe mark, one per board.

On small screens the current guess stays at the top and the keyboard stays at the bottom. The three history boards scroll in the middle. After each guess they re-rank: unsolved boards first, then most locked letters, then known-in-word letters, so the closest board snaps to the top. Solved boards compact and sink.

Site nav and heading still collapse behind the play-pull chrome used on sudoku.
