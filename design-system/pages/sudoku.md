# Sudoku

Classic 9×9 medium. Sticky pad with 1–9, notes, clear. Givens locked. Keyboard arrows and digits. Notes render as a 3×3 microgrid inside empty cells.

On small screens the site nav and heading slide away so the board and pad sit in view. The “Pad” label, keyboard hint, and back link hide; the 3×3 keys sit tighter under the grid.
